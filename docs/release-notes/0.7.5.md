## What changed

- This release shipped under the old name, win11-clipboard-history.
- The popup now picks the right tab before it shows on screen. Before, it could briefly show the wrong tab.

## What was fixed

- The emoji shortcut (Super+period) no longer clashes with the IBus emoji shortcut. The app frees it when it sets up the emoji shortcut.
- A bug in the installer's uninstall step is fixed. Uninstalling now also puts your IBus hotkey back.
- Extra timing logs can be switched on for people who debug popup speed. They are off by default.
