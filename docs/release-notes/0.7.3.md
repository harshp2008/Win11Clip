## What's new

- The popup can be placed at your cursor on Wayland, using spawn-at 0.2.3.
- The installer checks that the GNOME extensions loaded. After you log in, it tells you if they did.
- The installer works on its own, without needing source files.

## What changed

- The installer asks before it removes the old package. It keeps spawn-at by default and backs up window-calls.
- The popup is no longer moved again after spawn-at has placed it.
- Terminal paste is simpler, and window margins were adjusted.
- This release shipped under the old name, win11-clipboard-history.

## What was fixed

- The popup stays above other windows on a cold start.
- Rounded window borders are back. The always-on-top lookup on Wayland works again.
- Transparency is turned off only when the display uses an NVIDIA graphics card.
- Shortcuts no longer overwrite each other with an old command.
- Clipboard items no longer load out of line after the Setup Wizard.
- The installer asks you to log out only when window-calls really changed. The uninstaller finds the window-calls backup.

## Known issues and upgrading

- On Wayland, log out and back in once after the GNOME extensions are installed or updated.
- Other known issues: details not recorded.
